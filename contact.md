## One way in, for now

Open an issue on the repo. That's the whole process.

[Open an interest issue →](https://github.com/kousiknandy/rubberducking.club/issues/new?template=interest.yml)

It's a short form — what you're stuck on, your timezone, whether you'd rather talk or type. About a minute.

## Why a GitHub issue?

Because this is deliberately low-tech.

Scheduling links, calendar embeds and reminder emails are all easy to add and pointless to add before anyone wants the thing. An issue costs nothing to run and answers the only question that matters right now: does anybody actually want to be ducked?

The friction is real and I know it filters people out. If posting in public isn't for you, that's fair — tell a friend who would.

## What happens next

- **A handful of issues:** I book sessions by hand, in the thread. Slow, fine, works.
- **More than a handful:** a proper calendar lands on this page.
- **People who want to *be* the duck too:** the club gets a roster, you pick a duck, or two members pair up directly and skip me entirely. That's the version I actually want.

## No GitHub account?

You'll need one to open an issue — signing up is free and takes a minute. Or just catch me the next time we talk; word of mouth is a perfectly good API.
